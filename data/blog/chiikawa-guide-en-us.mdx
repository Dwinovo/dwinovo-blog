# What is this mod?

**Chiikawa Mod** is an unofficial Minecraft mod that drops the healing little creatures from the anime _Chiikawa_ straight into your world.

It isn't a "reskin pet" mod. Beyond the 7 wild characters that spawn around the world, the mod ships a full **taming system**, **profession system**, **doll-based revival ritual**, and — new in 0.0.4 — a complete **custom music performance system**. Once it's installed, your save permanently gains a new cast of companions: noisy at times, comforting at others, capable of farming, fighting alongside you, and now performing your favorite songs on guitar.

## 📥 How to Download

The mod is officially listed on **[CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/chiikawa)** — the cleanest path is to install it through a launcher rather than dropping the file in by hand. Most modern launchers (CurseForge App, Modrinth App, Prism, PCL2, HMCL) can search and install it directly from the mods browser.

## 🐾 The Cast

The mod adds **7 tameable companions** in total. They all share the same base stats (**20 HP, 0.3 movement speed**), but each has unique sound cues, and a few have exclusive weapons or jobs.

| Character     | A.k.a.                               | What makes them special                                                                                              |
| :------------ | :----------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Usagi**     | the bunny                            | Unique attack & ambient voice cues; signature weapon **Usagi's Stick** (Iron sword stats + Fire Aspect I)            |
| **Hachiware** | the blue one                         | **The only one who can become a Musician**; signature weapon **Hachiware's Stick** (Stone sword stats + Knockback I) |
| **Chiikawa**  | the protagonist                      | Signature weapon **Chiikawa's Stick** (Stone sword stats + Knockback I)                                              |
| **Shisa**     | the little lion                      | Cozy companion                                                                                                       |
| **Momonga**   | the flying squirrel who wants praise | Cozy companion                                                                                                       |
| **Kurimanju** | the round one                        | Cozy companion                                                                                                       |
| **Rakko**     | the mentor                           | Cozy companion                                                                                                       |

> In Creative mode, you'll find all 7 **spawn eggs**, 7 **dolls**, 3 **Subjugation Sticks**, and 1 **Music Box** inside the dedicated "Chiikawa" creative tab — 18 items total.

## 🌏 Where to Find Them

Wild Chiikawa spawn in these biomes, mostly warm and temperate regions:

- **Plains**: Plains, Sunflower Plains
- **Savanna**: Savanna, Savanna Plateau, Desert
- **Other**: Swamp, Snowy Plains

All 7 species share the same spawn rules and **spawn solo** (no packs) — every encounter is a one-of-a-kind moment.

## 💖 Taming & Daily Interaction

### 1. How to Tame

Hold a food item in your main hand and **right-click** a wild Chiikawa to feed it.

- **Success rate**: **30%** per feed.
- **Cost**: one food item per attempt, succeed or fail — bring a stack.
- **On success**: that character's unique tame sound plays, plus a happy little animation.
- **On failure**: it does a confused head-scratch instead — try again with another bite.

### 2. Accepted Foods

Almost any edible vanilla item works:

> 🍎 **Fruits & Vegetables**: Apple, Carrot, Golden Carrot, Melon Slice, Potato, Sweet Berries, Glow Berries, Beetroot
>
> 🍖 **Meats (Cooked)**: Steak, Cooked Chicken, Cooked Porkchop, Cooked Mutton, Cooked Rabbit, Cooked Cod, Cooked Salmon
>
> 🍞 **Baked Goods & Snacks**: Bread, Baked Potato, Cookie, Pumpkin Pie, Golden Apple
>
> 🥣 **Soups & Drinks**: Mushroom Stew, Rabbit Stew, Honey Bottle

### 3. After Taming

- **Healing**: any of the foods above can be fed to a tamed pet for **+4 HP** per item (consumed only if the pet is below max HP).
- **Backpack**: right-click your tamed companion to open its **16-slot** inventory panel.
- **Main-hand slot**: the first slot is the **equipment slot** — whatever tool sits here automatically determines the pet's active job.

> 💡 The mod **doesn't include breeding** — every companion is an individual you've personally tamed from the wild. Take good care of your party.

## 🧠 Three Behavior Modes

Crouch + right-click a tamed companion to cycle through three modes. The current mode flashes briefly on your action bar.

### 1. Follow Mode (default)

It tags along behind you like a small shadow.

- If the distance exceeds **7 blocks**, it sprints to catch up.
- If the distance exceeds **20 blocks**, it teleports to your side (no more "wait, where's my Hachiware…" moments after a Nether trip).

### 2. Sit Mode

Stays put — but its gaze still tracks nearby entities. You can feel it watching.

- Doesn't move, doesn't teleport.
- Perfect for parking it before a boss fight or while building delicate redstone.

### 3. Work Mode

The moment you flip to this mode, the pet locks its current position as its "workstation" and starts performing whatever its current job demands.

- When there's nothing to do, it wanders around its workstation — never straying far.
- See the next section for what each job actually does.

## 🛠️ Profession System

A pet's job is determined automatically by **whatever tool you put in its main hand**. Swap the tool, swap the job.

### 🌾 Farmer

**Requirement**: any **Hoe** in the main hand.

In Work mode, it will:

1. **Auto-harvest** fully-grown **Wheat, Potato, Carrot, Beetroot, Melon, and Pumpkin** blocks within range.
2. **Auto-replant** using seeds from its own backpack (Wheat Seeds, Beetroot Seeds, Pumpkin Seeds, Melon Seeds, Potato, Carrot).
3. **Auto-deposit** harvested crops into adjacent **Chests, Trapped Chests, Barrels, or Hoppers**.

Drop it into your village farm or your enclosed crop pen and you've got an automation line.

### ⚔️ Fencer

**Requirement**: any **Sword** in the main hand — including all six vanilla swords and the three mod Subjugation Sticks.

- Actively attacks nearby hostile mobs: zombies, skeletons, spiders, illagers (pillagers, vindicators…), blazes, ghasts, and other common monsters.
- Attack cadence is roughly one swing per second.

### 🏹 Archer

**Requirement**: a **Bow** in the main hand + **arrows** in the backpack.

- Same target list as the Fencer, but it keeps its distance and engages at range.
- Supports the **Infinity** enchantment — a single arrow is enough if the bow has it.

### 🎸 Musician <span className="ml-1 align-middle text-[10px] font-bold tracking-wide text-pink-600 dark:text-pink-400">NEW</span>

**Requirement**: **Hachiware only** — main hand holds a **Music Box** with a track already selected.

- In Work mode, Hachiware picks up his guitar and performs the chosen track. Nearby players hear it.
- During performance his guitar model appears and his mouth expression bone activates — he's playing for real.
- If a hostile mob attacks him mid-song, the performance pauses; once he's safe, he picks it back up.

> Full walkthrough below: [Music Box & Custom Music](#-music-box--custom-music).

## 🎵 Music Box & Custom Music

The headline feature of 0.0.4. Bring any song you want into Minecraft and let Hachiware perform it live for you and everyone nearby.

### 1. Get FFmpeg ready

The mod relies on **FFmpeg** to transcode arbitrary audio files into the in-game stream format.

- FFmpeg is a free one-time install on most platforms (on Windows, make sure it's on your PATH after installing).
- If the mod can't find FFmpeg, the Music Box UI shows a clear warning and a "Reload" button — just install FFmpeg and click reload, no restart required.
- Server admins can sanity-check the install with `/chiikawa music ffmpeg status`.

### 2. Drop in your tracks

Open the server's (or single-player world's) config directory and find `config/chiikawa/music/`. Drop in any common audio format:

> 🎼 **Supported formats**: mp3 / wav / flac / m4a / ogg — basically anything FFmpeg can decode.

The mod transcodes them in the background. Default per-track length cap is **10 minutes**, adjustable in `config/chiikawa/music.json`.

### 3. Pick a song from the Music Box

Hold the Music Box and **right-click** to open the song list:

- Successfully imported tracks are clickable — click to select.
- The item's name updates to show the current song (e.g. `Song: your-favorite.mp3`).
- Tracks still being processed show as **"Importing"**; failed conversions show as **"Failed"**.

### 4. Let Hachiware perform

1. Right-click your tamed Hachiware to open his backpack, then drag the **Music Box** into the **main-hand slot**.
2. Crouch + right-click to flip him into **Work Mode**.
3. He picks up the guitar and starts playing 🎶.

By default, every player within **32 blocks** of him hears the music — think of him as a walking, breathing BGM speaker.

### 5. Tips

- **Mini concerts**: hand different Hachiwares different songs and host a forest music festival. The default concurrent session cap is 4.
- **Combat priority**: if Hachiware is attacked mid-performance, he pauses; once the threat is gone, he resumes.
- **Admin command**: `/chiikawa music rescan` force-reloads the music folder, so newly added tracks become available without a server restart.

## 💀 Death & Revival: Doll + Cake Ritual

> ⚠️ This is a hidden mechanic — most players don't know about it. Once you've read this section, your pets never truly die.

### 1. What happens on death

When a tamed companion's HP hits zero, it doesn't disappear like a vanilla pet — **it drops a species-specific Doll item** on the spot.

That doll fully preserves the pet's:

- Entire **16-slot backpack contents**
- Current **behavior mode** (Follow / Sit / Work)
- **Job** assignment
- The **custom name** you gave it
- Its **ownership** bond with you

Death is, effectively, a temporary transformation into a portable doll — one you can revive later.

> 💡 Every doll has a quiet tooltip hint: _"Try placing the doll on a cake?"_ — that's the secret.

### 2. The revival ritual

Materials are simple:

- ✅ 1 **matching-species doll** (the one that dropped on death)
- ✅ 1 **Cake** (any bite state — fresh, half-eaten, doesn't matter)

Steps:

1. Place the cake.
2. Holding the doll, **right-click** the cake.
3. A bite is taken out of the cake (or the last slice is consumed); the doll is consumed.
4. **Green happy-villager particles + heart particles** appear above the cake for about **3 seconds**.
5. Your companion reappears at the cake — fully healed, all data intact, facing toward you.

> 💡 If the spawn position is blocked, the ritual aborts safely and **the doll is returned** to you — nothing is lost.

### 3. Creative mode note

In Creative mode, dolls aren't consumed on use — handy for testing. But remember: a doll stores **that specific companion's exact state**, not a fresh copy.

## ⚔️ The Three Subjugation Sticks

The mod includes signature weapons for Usagi, Hachiware, and Chiikawa. They replicate the anime's look and come pre-enchanted:

| Weapon Name           | Base Stats        | Enchantments     |
| :-------------------- | :---------------- | :--------------- |
| **Usagi's Stick**     | Iron Sword stats  | 🔥 Fire Aspect I |
| **Hachiware's Stick** | Stone Sword stats | 💨 Knockback I   |
| **Chiikawa's Stick**  | Stone Sword stats | 💨 Knockback I   |

The sticks count as swords, so they double as Fencer-job triggers — slip one into a pet's main hand and it gets both a thematic weapon and an active combat profession.
